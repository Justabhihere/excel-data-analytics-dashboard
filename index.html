📄 Marks file format guidance
Required columns: exactly BITS ID, Course and Total Marks. Column names are matched safely even if capitalization/extra spaces differ.
Marks: expected on a 0–100 scale. Whole-number marks are used for grading. Invalid, blank or out-of-range marks are rejected with a clear message.
NC students: students who did not appear and should receive NC should not be included in the uploaded file.
Privacy: processing happens locally in your browser; the app does not send your workbook to a server.
